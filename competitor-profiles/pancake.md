# Pancake - targeted competitor profile

**URL:** https://pancake.vn/
**Generated:** 2026-08-01
**Depth:** Workflow-focused scan

## Relevant strengths

- Strong Vietnam-first channel coverage including Facebook and Zalo.
- Mature operating taxonomy: filters, tags, rotation, permissions, staff statistics, canned replies and error handling.
- Separates customer CRM data from the conversation-management surface.

## Limits for this project

- Product is optimized heavily around social commerce and orders.
- Using it as the primary inbox would place another vendor between the clinic and customer conversations and would duplicate parts of ZenithTasks.

## What ZenithTasks should copy

- Simple per-channel connection flow.
- Combined queue with channel filters and tags.
- Clear configuration and error-recovery area.
