# SleekFlow - targeted competitor profile

**URL:** https://sleekflow.io/
**Generated:** 2026-08-01
**Depth:** Workflow-focused scan

## Relevant strengths

- Clear three-pane inbox model with contact context beside the thread.
- Explicit owner, team, collaborator and internal-note concepts.
- Granular permissions for view, reply, assignment and channel management.

## Limits for this project

- Broader sales and campaign scope than ZenithTasks needs initially.
- Advanced flow building would add unnecessary complexity to the first release.

## What ZenithTasks should copy

- One accountable owner per conversation.
- Internal notes that are visually distinct from customer messages.
- Customer context panel and role-separated channel administration.
