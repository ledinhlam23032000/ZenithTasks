# respond.io - targeted competitor profile

**URL:** https://respond.io/

## Relevant strengths

- Clean separation of Contacts, channel identities, Conversations and Messages.
- Strong identity merge, assignment, delivery-status and channel-troubleshooting behavior.
- Useful collision prevention through teammate typing presence.

## Limits for this project

- General-purpose messaging product rather than a clinic record system.
- Direct use would require maintaining two sources of truth for customers and staff permissions.

## What ZenithTasks should copy

- Unknown contact first, optional customer link later.
- Unassigned queue with first-response claim.
- Open/snoozed/closed lifecycle, internal events, message delivery states and permission refresh.
